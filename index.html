How I Think About Marketing

Marketing that doesn't move a number isn't marketing.
It's just activity.

I build programs around outcomes: pipeline, conversion, cost efficiency, revenue. Every channel decision and content investment traces back to what the business actually needs to grow.

I start with the buyer, map the strategy to where they are in the journey, and then execute with the same rigor I brought to building the strategy in the first place.

I've worked across B2B and B2C, in-house and as a consultant. I partner with sales, product, ops, executives, creative teams, contractors, and agencies to get campaigns out the door. What stays the same: be deliberate, be data-driven, and be accountable for the outcome, not just the output.

Endava had campaigns running. What they didn't have was a strategy connecting those campaigns to the buyer journey or a clear path from lead to pipeline. Syndication, nurture, lead routing, and follow-up were all operating independently. The opportunity was to build the connective tissue: a full-funnel demand generation program that tied activity to outcomes and gave sales qualified leads worth acting on.

  • Content syndication aligned to webinar registration and target audience

I built and ran the program end-to-end. Post-webinar nurture was structured around engagement signals. Attendees who stayed 40+ minutes received a different follow-up than those who dropped early. What results has Heather delivered? +

What is the difference between demand generation and ABM? +
Demand generation is a broad, full-funnel approach to creating awareness and pipeline across a target market. ABM targets specific high-value accounts with personalized messaging and coordinated sales and marketing outreach. Heather designs and executes both, often combining them into a tiered approach.
How does Heather measure marketing success? +
Against business outcomes, not just activity metrics. MQL-to-SQL conversion rates, pipeline influenced, cost per lead, lead-to-MQL conversion, and revenue contribution. Shared KPI frameworks between marketing and sales so both teams are measuring the same thing.
